Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

KPI Master ("we", "our", or "us") helps teams visualize key performance indicators and workflow efficiency directly from Trello boards and on our web application. This Privacy Policy explains what data is accessed, processed, stored, and shared when you use the KPI Master Power-Up or web dashboard. Please read it together with the privacy notices of the third-party services listed below.

1. Data Minimization & How We Access Data

When you authorize KPI Master, it accesses Trello data needed for the features you use through Trello's API and Power-Up interface. Depending on the feature, this can include board and list names and IDs, card names and IDs, assigned member IDs and names, activity dates, comments, and contact history. The app uses this information to display dashboards, calculate metrics, and perform actions you request in Trello.

KPI calculations are performed in your browser. Trello data is requested from Trello when a feature needs it; KPI Master does not operate a database for storing Trello board or card contents.

2. Personal Data Storage

KPI Master does not operate a database for Trello board or card contents. Some information is stored in your browser or in Trello Power-Up storage so the features you choose can work.

  • Browser storage: The web dashboard stores its Trello access token, selected board, role, preferences, and locally calculated activity-time information in your browser. The activity-time information can include a Trello member ID, display name, and time totals. Logging out clears the dashboard's localStorage for this site. You can also clear this site's browser data in your browser settings.
  • Trello Power-Up storage: The Power-Up stores settings and message templates, and can store contact counts and history on cards. Contact history includes the action type, date/time, outcome, and the acting member's display name. Card and board data saved with shared visibility can be seen by people who can view that card or board. Member-private data is stored in Trello for that member.
  • Disconnecting and deletion: Logging out clears the dashboard's local browser storage. Revoking authorization stops future API access, but does not necessarily remove data already saved in Trello Power-Up storage or data previously sent to third-party services. Contact us to request help locating or deleting data that KPI Master controls; browser and Trello storage may also need to be cleared separately.

3. Product Analytics (PostHog and Vercel)

We use product analytics to understand feature adoption and improve the service. The Trello Power-Up and web dashboard send explicitly defined interaction event names to PostHog Cloud EU. The dashboard web app also uses Vercel Web Analytics for page-view and visitor statistics. These are separate analytics services and are not used to send Trello card contents as event properties.

  • PostHog events: We send fixed event names for selected interactions, such as opening a feature or changing a setting. Before sending, the app removes event properties, so properties passed by feature code (including board, card, or member IDs, names, text, and contact details) are not included in these custom PostHog events. Automatic page views, automatic click/input capture, and session recording are disabled.
  • Cookieless PostHog measurement: PostHog is configured with cookieless mode and does not use browser cookies or local/session storage for its analytics identity. When the required “Cookieless server hash mode” is enabled in the PostHog project settings, PostHog's servers calculate a privacy-preserving daily hash using request information such as IP address, browser user agent, and hostname. The hash changes daily; this configuration does not use it to identify a person across days. See PostHog's cookieless tracking documentation.
  • Vercel Web Analytics: On the web application, Vercel records page views and provides aggregated information such as page/route, referrer, country, browser, operating system, and device category. Vercel states that it uses an anonymized daily visitor hash and does not use cookies for Web Analytics. This analytics is not the PostHog feature-event tracker. See Vercel Web Analytics documentation.
  • No direct Trello identity in PostHog: KPI Master does not call PostHog's user-identification API or attach Trello member, board, or card identifiers to the custom PostHog events. Analytics providers still receive the network request needed to deliver their services and may process technical request information as described in their own notices.
  • Graceful Degradation: If telemetry is blocked by ad-blockers or privacy extensions, the Power-Up continues to operate; analytics are not required for its core features.

4. Third-Party Integrations

Feedback: When you submit the Trello feedback wizard, its rating, selected answers, free-text suggestions, timestamp, and locale are sent to a Make.com webhook so we can review the feedback. The web dashboard feedback form sends the text you submit to a Make.com webhook. Neither form automatically attaches your Trello account identity, but free text can identify you if you include your name, email, customer details, or other identifying information. Do not include confidential card or customer data. Make.com processes this information under its own terms and privacy notice.

User-triggered exports: If you configure a Make.com or Zapier webhook and choose to export a report, the report information selected or generated for that action is sent to the service configured by you. The receiving service's privacy and retention practices then apply.

Other providers: The app also makes requests to Trello to provide the integration. The Power-Up and web application load some fonts and libraries from third-party delivery services; those services receive standard connection information when your browser requests those resources.

5. Revoking Access & Disconnecting

You maintain complete control over your account. You can disconnect KPI Master or revoke its access token at any time:

  • Inside Trello: Open Power-Up Settings → Disconnect Account to revoke access.
  • Through your Atlassian account: Navigate to your Atlassian Account Settings → Connected Apps → Revoke KPI Master.

6. Contact & Data Protection Inquiries

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access, correction, or deletion of information handled by KPI Master, please contact us. Requests concerning Trello or analytics-provider data may also require contacting those providers or clearing the relevant Trello/browser storage.
