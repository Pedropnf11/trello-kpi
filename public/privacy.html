Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

KPI Master ("we", "our", or "us") helps teams visualize key performance indicators and workflow efficiency directly from Trello boards and on our web application. This Privacy Policy explains what data is accessed, processed, and stored when you use the KPI Master Power-Up or our web dashboard, in strict compliance with the Atlassian Developer Terms, Public Power-Up Guidelines, and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Minimization & How We Access Data

When you authorize KPI Master via Trello's client authorization, we dynamically retrieve only the board-related data necessary to compute metrics in real time through the official Trello API. This includes board names, lists, cards, assigned member IDs, activity dates, and comments.

We operate on a client-side processing architecture: calculations (such as lead speeds, conversion funnels, and card age badges) are performed dynamically in your browser session.

2. Personal Data Storage & GDPR Compliance

We do not store Trello user personal data on our servers.

  • No External Server Databases: We do not operate external databases storing confidential card details, card descriptions, or member personal identifying information outside of Trello.
  • Local Browser Storage: Some non-sensitive user preferences (such as selected language, board IDs, and feature toggles) may be stored locally in your browser's localStorage or directly inside Trello's secure storage via the Power-Up Plugin API (t.set()).
  • Right to Erasure & Deletion: Because no personal data is retained on external servers, disconnecting the Power-Up immediately removes access to your board data. Users can request total deletion of any support correspondence or local data records at any time by contacting us.

3. Telemetry & Analytics (PostHog)

To improve application performance and understand feature adoption, KPI Master collects aggregated, anonymized usage telemetry using PostHog Inc. (hosted in the European Union - EU Cloud).

  • Strictly Defined Events: We only track explicitly defined user interaction events (such as opening the dashboard popup, switching languages, or clicking the web dashboard button).
  • No Session Recording: Screen and session recording is strictly disabled (disable_session_recording: true) to prevent any accidental capture of board contents.
  • No Autocapture: Automatic click scraping and text input capturing are disabled (autocapture: false). Card titles, card descriptions, confidential comments, and API tokens are never tracked or sent to analytics.
  • Graceful Degradation: If telemetry is blocked by ad-blockers or privacy extensions, the Power-Up continues to operate with 100% of its functionality unaffected.

4. Third-Party Integrations

KPI Master allows users to optionally export reports to external workflow tools like Make.com or Zapier when configured by the user. We only transmit the aggregated summary data specified by you when you trigger an export action.

5. Revoking Access & Disconnecting

You maintain complete control over your account. You can disconnect KPI Master or revoke its access token at any time:

  • Inside Trello: Open Power-Up Settings → Disconnect Account.
  • Through your Atlassian account: Navigate to your Atlassian Account Settings → Connected Apps → Revoke KPI Master.
